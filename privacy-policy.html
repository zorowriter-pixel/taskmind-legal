Privacy Policy

TaskMind — Last updated: August 12, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskMind (the "app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the mobile application.

1. Information TaskMind Collects

TaskMind collects the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, and profile photo (when signing in with Google or Email/Password)
  • User content: Tasks, habits, notes, categories, and chat messages you create in the app
  • Usage data: App activity such as habit completions, task completions, and streaks
  • Voice input (optional): When you use voice input, audio is processed on-device by your phone's speech recognition system. Audio recordings are not stored.

2. How Your Information Is Used

  • To provide and improve the app's features (tasks, habits, AI assistant, insights)
  • To sync your data across sessions via Firebase
  • To personalize responses from the AI assistant (Orbit)
  • To send notifications you have opted into (habit reminders, streak alerts)

3. Third-Party Services

TaskMind uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, cloud database (Firestore), and analytics. Firebase Privacy
  • Groq API: Powers the Orbit AI assistant. Chat messages are sent to Groq for processing. Groq Privacy
  • Google Sign-In: Optional login method. Google Privacy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers. Access is restricted to your account only — TaskMind uses Firestore security rules to ensure no other user can access your data. Data is transmitted using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (see section 7), all your personal data is permanently removed from TaskMind's servers within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (visible in the app at any time)
  • Correct or update your data (edit tasks, habits, profile)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of notifications (in app settings)

7. Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, please visit the Account Deletion page or contact the developer at the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

TaskMind is not intended for users under 13 years of age. Personal information from children is not knowingly collected.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
