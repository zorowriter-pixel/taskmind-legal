TaskMind — Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your TaskMind account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, email, photo)
  • All tasks, habits, and notes
  • All categories you created
  • Chat history with Orbit AI
  • Streak data and habit completions
  • App preferences and settings
⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please export any data you wish to keep before proceeding.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 — From within the app (recommended)

  1. Open TaskMind on your phone
  2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom-right)
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted

Option 2 — By email request

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request from the email address associated with your account:

Email: zoro.writer@gmail.com

Subject line: TaskMind Account Deletion Request

Include: The email address of the account you want deleted

Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days, with a confirmation sent via email when complete.

Data Retention After Deletion

Once your account is deleted:

  • All personal data is removed from the active database immediately
  • Backups are purged within 30 days
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage data (with no personal identifiers) may be retained for analytics purposes

Third-Party Data

If you signed in using Google, deleting your TaskMind account does not affect your Google account. To revoke TaskMind's access to your Google account, visit Google Account Permissions.
